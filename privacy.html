Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

PGM Marketing is a small business based in Ontario, Canada, run by Paolo Martinuzzi. This page explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, what we do with it, and what choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and in plain language.

We follow Canada's federal privacy law, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), and Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) for the emails and text messages we send.

Who to contact

Paolo Martinuzzi is responsible for how we handle personal information. If you have a question about this policy, want to see or correct your information, or have a concern, email paolo.pmconsulting@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you contact us. If you fill in the contact form on our website or email us, we collect the details you give us, such as your name, email address, business name, the kind of work you do, and your message.

Business contact details from public sources. To let local service businesses know about our services, we look at publicly available business information, such as a business name, website, public business email address, phone number, address, and its public Google rating and review count. This is information businesses publish so that customers can reach them.

Our emails with you. If we exchange emails, we keep those messages so we can serve you and follow up.

Text messages for our business clients. If a business uses our missed call text back service, we also handle information about people who call that business. See the section on text messages below.

Our website does not use advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To reply to your messages and answer your questions.
  • To contact local service businesses about help we can offer.
  • To deliver and manage work we have agreed to do for a client.
  • To keep basic records of who we have contacted, so we do not contact someone twice by mistake.

We use personal information only for the reasons we gave when we collected it. If we want to use it for a new reason, we will ask you first. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Our outreach emails

Any email we send to a business is sent to a business address that is publicly listed, relates to that business's services, and identifies who we are. Every message can be answered with a request to stop, and we will honour that request right away, and in every case within 10 business days, and keep you off our list. Just reply "stop" or email us at the address above.

Text messages (missed call text back)

Our missed call text back service works for a business that has signed up with us. When a call to that business goes unanswered, the caller gets a text message from that business asking what they need and when. We send the text only in response to a call to the business.

  • What we handle. The caller's phone number, the time of the call, and the text messages that go back and forth.
  • Why. Only to send the text, to pass the caller's reply to the business so they can call back, and to keep the service working. The business owner sees the replies on their own phone.
  • Who the message is from. Each text names the business it is sent for, so you can tell who is contacting you.
  • Stopping texts. Reply STOP to any text and no more will be sent to your number. We honour this right away, and in every case within 10 business days.
  • No other use. We do not use callers' numbers or messages for our own marketing, and we do not sell them.

We act on the business's behalf for these texts. If you have a question about a text you received, you can ask the business that sent it, or email us at the address above.

Google and Gmail data

We use an internal tool to send our outreach from our own Gmail account and to notice when someone replies, so we can stop sending follow ups. The tool connects to Google only with our own account and only after we approve it.

  • We use this access only to send our own emails and to detect replies from the people we have contacted.
  • We do not sell Gmail data, share it with other companies, or use it for advertising.
  • No person other than the account owner reads Gmail messages through this tool.

Our use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Who else handles information

We use a small number of outside services to run the business. They process information only to provide their service to us.

  • Formspree receives what you type into our website contact form and forwards it to our email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Google provides our email, and the Maps and Places information about local businesses.
  • Anthropic provides the AI service we use to help draft the wording of messages. We give it public business details, such as a business name, city, and review count, to do this.
  • A text messaging and phone service provider sends and receives the texts for our missed call text back service.

Some of these services store information outside Canada, including in the United States, so that information may be subject to the laws of those places, including access by courts and authorities there.

How long we keep it

We keep contact and email records for as long as they are useful for the work we do, and then delete them. We keep callers' numbers and text messages only as long as the business needs them to follow up, and delete them when the business stops using the service. If you ask us to delete your information, we will, except where we are required to keep a record, such as a note that you asked us not to contact you again.

Your choices and your rights

You can ask us at any time to show you the information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop contacting you. You can also withdraw your consent. Email us at the address above. We will answer within 30 days. We may need to check who you are before we share information.

If you are not happy with how we handled your request or your information, tell us first and we will try to put it right. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. You do not need a lawyer to do that.

Keeping information safe

We use reasonable safeguards, such as password protected accounts and access limited to the business owner. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to protect what you share with us. If a security breach puts your personal information at real risk of significant harm, we will report it to the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and tell you, as the law requires. We keep a record of every breach for at least 24 months.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
