Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

PGM Marketing is a small marketing business based in Ontario, Canada, run by Paolo Martinuzzi. This page explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. We have tried to keep it short and in plain language.

Who to contact

If you have any question about this policy or your information, email paolo.pmconsulting@gmail.com. That is the best way to reach us about privacy.

What we collect

When you contact us. If you fill in the contact form on our website or email us, we collect the details you give us, such as your name, email address, business name, and your message.

Business contact details from public sources. To let local service businesses know about our services, we look at publicly available business information, such as a business name, website, public business email address, phone number, address, and its public Google rating and review count. This is information businesses publish so that customers can reach them.

Our emails with you. If we exchange emails, we keep those messages so we can serve you and follow up.

Our website does not use advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To reply to your messages and answer your questions.
  • To contact local service businesses about marketing help we can offer.
  • To deliver and manage work we have agreed to do for a client.
  • To keep basic records of who we have contacted, so we do not contact someone twice by mistake.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Our outreach emails

Any email we send to a business is sent to a business address that is publicly listed, relates to that business's services, and identifies who we are. Every message can be answered with a request to stop, and we will honour that request right away and keep you off our list. Just reply "stop" or email us at the address above.

Google and Gmail data

We use an internal tool to send our outreach from our own Gmail account and to notice when someone replies, so we can stop sending follow ups. The tool connects to Google only with our own account and only after we approve it.

  • We use this access only to send our own emails and to detect replies from the people we have contacted.
  • We do not sell Gmail data, share it with other companies, or use it for advertising.
  • No person other than the account owner reads Gmail messages through this tool.

Our use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Who else handles information

We use a small number of outside services to run the business. They process information only to provide their service to us.

  • Formspree receives what you type into our website contact form and forwards it to our email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Google provides our email, and the Maps and Places information about local businesses.
  • Anthropic provides the AI service we use to help draft the wording of messages. We give it public business details, such as a business name, city, and review count, to do this.

Some of these services store information outside Canada, including in the United States, so that information may be subject to the laws of those places.

How long we keep it

We keep contact and email records for as long as they are useful for the work we do, and then delete them. If you ask us to delete your information, we will, except where we are required to keep a record, such as a note that you asked us not to contact you again.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you the information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop contacting you. Email us at the address above. If you are not happy with how we handled your request, you can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Keeping information safe

We use reasonable safeguards, such as password protected accounts and access limited to the business owner. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to protect what you share with us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
